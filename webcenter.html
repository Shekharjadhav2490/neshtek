Our upgrade method
  1. Discover — topology, versions, customizations and integrations.
  2. Assess — compatibility, risk and dependency analysis.
  3. Dry run — execute the upgrade in lower environments.
  4. Cut over — controlled production migration with rollback plan.
  5. Stabilize — validation, tuning, monitoring and knowledge transfer.
